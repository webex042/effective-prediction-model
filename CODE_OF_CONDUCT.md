# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want EPM to be a place where anyone can ask a question about the
mathematics, challenge a result, or submit a first pull request without being
made to feel small for it. Everyone who takes part — maintainers, contributors,
people filing issues — is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This split leaks test data into training"
  is useful; "this is amateur work" is not.
- Accept correction gracefully. Being wrong about a result is normal in a
  research project; defending a wrong result is not.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience. A beginner's
  question is not noise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including that relating to age,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing issues.
- Fabricating or knowingly misrepresenting experimental results.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **ranpranshu65@gmail.com**.
Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially, and the reporter's
identity will not be shared without their consent.

The maintainer may warn, remove comments or commits, or block a contributor
from the repository, depending on severity. Enforcement decisions are the
maintainer's to make, and will be explained to the person affected.
